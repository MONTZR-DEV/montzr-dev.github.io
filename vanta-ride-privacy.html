Privacy Policy

VANTA RIDEcom.montzrdev.vantarideLast updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MONTZR-DEV ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you play VANTA RIDE.

1. Information we collect

  • Google Play Games account: when you sign in, we receive your Google Play Games player identifier and a one-time authorization code used only to create and sign in to your game account. We never receive your Google password.
  • Game profile: your chosen display name, a unique player ID generated by the game, avatar and game progress (coins, bikes, upgrades, settings).
  • Online and social data: ranked points (RP) and tier, match results, friends list and friend requests.
  • Guest accounts: if you play as a guest, an anonymous identifier is created for your device.
  • Device and technical data: device model, OS version, IP address and diagnostic/crash information needed to run online matches.
  • Advertising data: if ads are shown, our advertising partners may collect the device advertising ID and ad interaction data.

2. How we use information

  • Create, sign in and save your account and progress across devices.
  • Run online races, matchmaking, rankings and the friends system (search by name or ID).
  • Prevent cheating and abuse, and fix bugs.
  • Show ads and measure their performance.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Games Services — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code) — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Photon (Exit Games) online multiplayer — photonengine.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob and advertising partners — policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your display name, player ID, rank and online status are visible to other players. Data is shared with the providers above only to operate the game.

5. Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit. We keep account data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, progress, rankings and friends data are deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your choices

You can change your display name in Settings, sign out at any time, and delete your account in the game (Settings → Delete account) or via our account deletion page.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
